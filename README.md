# Barn Management

A comprehensive farm management application built with **WinForms**.  
The system provides full management of **animals, products, sales, and user accounts**, including password encryption and detailed transaction logging.

## Features

- **Login/Signup / User Management:**  
  Users can create accounts, log in securely with encrypted passwords, or access the system with admin privileges.

- **Menu & Navigation:**  
  The application consists of 7 main forms:
  - **Log In**
  - **Sign Up**
  - **Menu**
  - **Animals**
  - **Products**
  - **Sales**
  - **Settings**

- **Animal Management:**  
  - Purchase animals with automatic balance verification:  
    - Cow: 70$  
    - Sheep: 35$  
    - Chicken: 10$  
    - Dog: 40$  
  - Animal ages increase over time (every 40 seconds).  
  - Lifespan & product generation:  
    - Cow: 20 years → produces **Milk**  
    - Sheep: 10 years → produces **Wool**  
    - Chicken: 5 years → produces **Egg**  
    - Dog: Provides protection → increases lifespan of other animals:
      - 1 dog: +2 years to all animals  
      - 2 dogs: +4 years  
      - 3+ dogs: +5 years  

- **Product Management:**  
  - View and sell products (**Milk, Wool, Egg**) in real-time.  
  - Product quantities automatically increase when animals reach maturity.  
  - Product sales update balances and record transactions with details including:
    - Operation type  
    - Amount  
    - Current balance  
    - User ID  
    - Timestamp

- **Sales Management:**  
  - Load money into the account.  
  - View all transactions with detailed history and user-specific information.  

- **Settings:**  
  - Enable/disable background music.  
  - Return to login screen.  
  - Exit application completely.

## How It Works

1. Start the application and log in or sign up.  
2. Navigate to the **Animals** form to purchase or monitor animals.  
3. Products generated by animals are automatically tracked and can be viewed or sold in the **Products** form.  
4. Load money and track transactions in the **Sales** form.  
5. Adjust music or application settings in the **Settings** form.
